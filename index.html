Brand story

Made by four students who were tired of the same black case.

Caseloom started as a Digital Commerce project at ATLAS Skilltech University (ISME). We wanted to test how a small brand can sell something personal without holding any stock.

The intended model is print-on-demand: make each case after an order instead of holding inventory. A print partner has not yet been selected, so this is a proposed model rather than a live fulfilment service.

Project status (30 October 2026): This is a student concept storefront. The six designs and customizer are demonstrations; there is no live checkout, payment, order fulfilment, verified product fit, customer review programme or active email-signup system.
